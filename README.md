## Why Blakcat

- 🔒 **Always secure.** Café, hotel, and airport Wi-Fi all become safe to use. Your traffic is encrypted the whole way — nobody in between can see where you go.
- 🌍 **Go anywhere.** Our own nodes cover every major region. Search, video, social, and developer services work out of the box, with no per-site setup to fiddle with.
- 🧭 **Smart routing.** Local sites go direct, everything else goes through the encrypted tunnel — decided for you. Speed or privacy isn't a trade-off, and you pick which regions stay direct.
- 🔄 **Always on.** Dropped connections retry themselves, the tunnel comes back after your device wakes, and a failing node is swapped for a healthy one. Leave it running in the background and you're protected without opening the app.
- 📱 **One experience everywhere.** The same core on every platform, one account for all your devices, and nothing to relearn when you switch machines.

## Feedback

**This issue tracker is for bugs** — crashes, a frozen UI, connected but no
traffic, no reconnect after sleep. For help using Blakcat (installing, account,
subscription, slow speeds) Telegram or email will answer you much faster.

| | |
|---|---|
| [Report a bug](../../issues/new/choose) | Reproducible problems. Include version and platform |
| [@blakcat_support](https://t.me/blakcat_support) | **Usage questions and support** |
| support@blakcat.app | Same, by email |
| [Discussions](../../discussions) | Feature requests, product discussion |
| [@blakcat_app](https://t.me/blakcat_app) | Announcements |

> ⚠️ Issues are public. **Do not paste logs containing node addresses, IPs,
> accounts or keys** — send those by Telegram or email instead.
> For security vulnerabilities see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
