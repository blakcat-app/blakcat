# Frequently asked questions

[English](FAQ.md) | [简体中文](FAQ.zh-CN.md)

## How does Blakcat work?

Blakcat creates an encrypted connection between your device and our own servers. Your traffic is encrypted before it leaves your device, so neither whoever runs the network you're on nor anyone else on the same Wi-Fi can see what you're doing.

With smart routing on, local sites connect directly without a detour, and only the rest goes through the encrypted tunnel — you don't have to trade speed for privacy.

## How do I install and use Blakcat?

Pick your platform on the download page and install the app. Sign in, choose a server, and tap connect. That's it.

Windows and Android can be downloaded directly, iOS is currently available as a TestFlight beta, and macOS is coming soon.

## Is Blakcat free?

Yes. We will always keep free servers running, and a free account is all you need. There are no ads in the app, and no third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs built in.

The free plan works on one device. Upgrading unlocks more servers and lets you sign in on several devices at once.

## Can I watch YouTube, Netflix, and other streaming services?

Yes. Once connected, YouTube, Netflix, Disney+ and other major streaming platforms work as usual, showing the catalog for the region your server is in.

For smooth HD streaming, use a Plus server or above.

## What is smart routing?

Smart routing decides how each connection should travel: local sites and services connect directly, and only traffic headed to other regions goes through the encrypted tunnel. Local video and apps stay fast, and you never have to switch anything by hand to reach services abroad.

You choose which regions connect directly under Direct region in settings.

## How secure is Blakcat?

All traffic is protected by the same grade of encryption your bank uses, and every server is deployed and run by us — no resold third-party capacity.

We don't log the websites you visit, your DNS queries, or the content of your traffic, and we never sell data. See our privacy policy for details.

## What happens if my connection drops?

Nothing you need to handle. The app reconnects on its own, switches to a healthy server in the same region if one goes down, and restores the connection when your device wakes from sleep.

On Android you can turn on the system's Always-on VPN, and on iOS the connection comes back by itself if it drops — you stay protected without opening the app.

## How many devices can I use?

The free plan covers one device, and members can be signed in on several devices at once. One account works across every platform, and you can remove a device you no longer use from Device management in the app.

## Is it legal to use Blakcat?

Using encryption tools to protect your privacy is legal in most countries and regions, but some places restrict it. Please use Blakcat in line with the laws where you are.
